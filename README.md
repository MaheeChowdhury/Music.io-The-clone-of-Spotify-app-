# Music.io-The-clone-of-Spotify-app-
Hello There, This is an Online Music Player app called Music.io, an alternative of our favourite spotify music app.

The key features that differs my app from the official spotify app are:
-You can play the music of my website directly through the website (No login, 2FA Authorization or verification needed) just play and listen method
-No Log in required (Even though I have put the Sign up and Login to make it look like the real spotify app, The login and sign up methods will be added up later even though it is not required.
-No modern Frameworks were used to build up this website, It was written in fully raw HTML<CSS and Javascript.The main purpose of doing this was to showcase the basic HTML,CSS and Javascript skills through raw coding even we're living in the modern age of Frameworks.

The main and challenging portion :
-The most challenging portion of building this project was doing everything raw handed, the hard part for me as a beginner of my first complete project was to Add the song properly into an album so that users could click on those albums and play the specific author's songs.Also, playing the songs one by one,adding a volume bar, getting it a range value using basic mathematical skills also put me in a little bit of struggle, but Alhamdulillah I've overcome with all of those.Also I've iptimized this website to be responsive for all of the android and IOS devices specially Iphones.The left sidebar will be opened after clicking the hamburger sign at the top left corner of the movile screens (just for phones, not pc).Also a user can listen to their favourite songs anytime they want.

I hope that my first project will make you very much satisfied, specially for those who are song lovers. Have fun.......(More songs will be added and ANOTHER PROJECT IS COMING IN FUTURE IN SHA ALLAH)
